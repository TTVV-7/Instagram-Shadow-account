## Caption
From a chat message to a print-ready file 🤖➡️🖨️

Meshy just released an official plugin for Hermes Agent, the open-source AI assistant from Nous Research. You describe what you want in a chat, and it:

✅ Generates a textured 3D model from text or images
✅ Checks if it's actually printable
✅ Repairs common geometry issues
✅ Scales it to real-world size
✅ Exports a 3MF, single- or multicolor, ready for your slicer

The plugin is free to install, but generating models needs a paid Meshy plan and uses credits. It asks you to approve the credit cost before each job runs.

My take: the hardest part of AI 3D models has always been the cleanup before you can print them. If the repair step holds up, that's the real story here. We're going to test it. 👀
