## Caption
Raise3D's new printer is a metre tall 📏🖨️

On Nov 11, Raise3D announced the T450 and T700: large-format printers with an actively heated chamber, which it calls the most affordable of their kind.

📦 T450: 450 × 350 × 400 mm, chamber up to 70°C, US$4,999
🏗️ T700: 700 × 500 × 1,000 mm, chamber up to 60°C, US$15,999
🔥 420°C hot end, 120°C bed, fibre-ready for PET CF, PPA CF and PPS CF
🔀 TwinFlow: two filaments into one nozzle, for speed or dissolvable supports

T450 deliveries start by the end of December, the T700 by the end of January. Both are at Formnext in Frankfurt, Nov 17–20.

Our take: it's a business machine, not a hobby upgrade. Most of our jobs fit on our Bambu and Snapmaker beds, but for one-piece parts this big, a printer like this beats cutting them up.
